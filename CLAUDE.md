Portfolio site for Susie (Yuyue Zhou), a pianist (B.M. Piano Performance, Manhattan School of Music) applying to Fall 2027 master's programs in music technology / audio engineering (CMU, UMich, Stanford CCRMA, Georgia Tech, NYU). Her goal is audio engineering: spatial audio and auditory perception.

- She often writes in Chinese. Answer in the language she uses. Site text is English.
- Site English must be simple, direct and natural: short sentences, no fancy phrasing, nothing that sounds AI-written or sentimental.

## Honesty rules (important for admissions)
- **Lost & Sound** was made with her teacher **Marc Evanstein**. Marc, working with AI (Claude), set up the template, fixed the coordinate system, and wrote the air-absorption and front/back filter functions. Susie wrote most of the audio features (movement, synthesis experiments, enemy voices + proximity speed-up, the layered home voice and its curves, tuning the front/back values, room settings, game art). Never overstate her role.
- AI (Claude): Marc worked with it on PLAN.md and his parts of the code. Susie used it as a tutor only (gitignored `.role` file = `teacher`/`student` switches how Claude helps); she wrote her parts herself. The site's "How we used AI" section explains this.
- **Flowing Light**: Susie composed the music and made the video. The images in the video were generated by AI from her prompts. Say so plainly.
- **The Lost & Sound game code can be public** — Marc agreed (Nov 2026). It may go in this repo and on the site.

## Files
All site files live in `docs/` (GitHub Pages serves from /docs) (paths below are relative to it). CLAUDE.md, README.md and .gitignore stay at the repo root.
- `lost-and-sound/` (repo root) — git submodule of the game repo (github.com/Yuyue777/SoundGame). The site links to that repo's own GitHub Pages; the game is not served from `docs/`. It has its own CLAUDE.md/PLAN.md.
- `index.html` — all pages. Views are `<main data-view="...">` sections routed by the URL hash: `#home`, `#scamp-pieces`, `#lost-and-sound`, `#flowing-light`, `#cv`.
- `css/style.css` — all styles. Colors are CSS variables in `:root` (light + dark via `light-dark()`); pink accent `--pink: #ff5fa8`, plum `--plum`. Home/header/footer use `--p-ink #1d1a1f`, `--p-mut #6f6873`, `--p-rule #d9d4dc`.
- `fonts/` — TeX Gyre Schola regular + italic (.otf, from GUST) for home titles. Body text on home is Inter.
- `js/main.js` — hash routing, the home-sound gain chart (Lost & Sound page), score galleries + lightbox, video chapter buttons, Copy buttons.
- Media: `flowing.mp4` + `poster.jpg` (Flowing Light), `scamp_performance.mp4` + `scamp_poster.jpg` (Susie playing the three SCAMP pieces; title cards cut out), `portrait.png` (CV photo).
- Scores as page images: `score/p1–p8.webp` (Flowing Light), `scamp/clown-1`, `qa-1..2`, `bunnies-1..3` `.webp`.
